    About Me

    Welcome to my page! I'm Marcus.

    I've been drawing ever since I was little, eventually deciding to make a life centered around my creative endeavors. I went to SCAD, graduating with a Bachelor’s of Fine Arts, majoring in Animation.

    I would later serve in the Navy for the next 6 years where I would later realize I want to continue my career as an artist. Eventually I would branch out into doing graphical design, even making my own comics.

    What you see here is a website I went and vibe-coded together to house all my works both professional and personal to show off what I can do.
